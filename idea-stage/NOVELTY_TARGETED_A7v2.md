## Findings

### 1. The "≤ 2M templates" fact is known mathematics — cite it, do not claim it

Maximizing |Σ_n s_n v_n|² over signs s ∈ {±1}ⁿ with complex v_n is a **rank-2 binary quadratic form**. Its optimum lies in an O(n) candidate set generated by an auxiliary angle (the sign of Re(e^{−jθ} v_n) changes only at arg v_n ± π/2), and it is solvable in O(n log n):

A PASS/communications reviewer who knows the Karystinos line (a CDMA spreading-code and ML-detection topic) would recognize it.

### 2. What remains new in Theorem 2 / Corollary 1 (no collision found)

- **Family-wide sharing.** The sign templates built from the breakpoints of **all M candidate sites** cover the endpoint-leakage maximum of **every** N-subset of the finite family at once, including the subset-dependent offset Σ_{n∈S} m_n. This holds because a zonotope's vertex sign patterns depend only on the generator directions, and a subset's angular cells coarsen the full arrangement. The rank-2 literature optimizes a single fixed vector set; none of the works found reuses one candidate set across a combinatorial family.
- **From leakage maxima to feasible SLNR witnesses.** Each template is applied as one physical error vector to **both** D and P, so U_H(S) ≥ W(S) holds for every subset with no endpoint-optimality assumption on the SLNR.
- **What this enables:** safe screening of the certified objective, exact certificate maximization over the family, the global bracket L(Ŝ) ≤ W* ≤ U*, and the sufficient uniqueness test — for robust PASS site selection under actuator boxes.
- **"Safe screening" is borrowed terminology.** Cite L. El Ghaoui, V. Viallon, T. Rabbani, "Safe Feature Elimination for the LASSO and Sparse Supervised Learning Problems," arXiv:1009.4219 (verified: arXiv listing in search results) — or say "safe pruning" and cite branch-and-bound bounding generically.
